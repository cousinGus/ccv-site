# Donate
## [Donate Here](https://www.zeffy.com/en-US/donation-form/cultivating-community-food-sovereignty-for-all)

When you make a contribution you're joining a collaboration of others who believe, as we do, in food sovereignty and put your voice forward in support of a community-focused food system.

### How you can help:
**Donate**: Although one-time contributions are still welcomed, we encourage smaller monthly donations to make your giving accessible and sustainable. Pause or cancel anytime should your circumstances ever change.  
  
**Share**: Spread the word to your friends, family, and on social media. Our Instagram account is [@foodsovereigntyforall](https://www.instagram.com/foodsovereigntyforall/)  
  
**Volunteer**: Take part in learning how to grow food, feed your neighbors, and beautify your city. Learn more on the [Volunteer page]({{ site.baseurl }}/volunteer)
