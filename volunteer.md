Cultivating Community uses [timecounts](https://timecounts.app/cultivating-community) for volunteer coordination

### Opportunities

**Sunday Crew**  
We're always happy to have volunteers who can help with food distribution near Columbia Heights or help pick up donated food around DC and Maryland. 


**Farmhand**  
Learn to grow organic healthy food!  
  
**Transporter**  
Deliver food donations to our organization!  

**Distributor**  
Give away free food to the community!  
  
**Interpreter**  
Communicate to give representation to local cultures!  
  
**Ambassador**   
Foster connections with local organizations and leaders!

We are also happy to have help in any of these areas

 - Fundraising
 - Operations
 - Logistics
 - Events
 - Marketing
