  <p>An avid gardener and aspiring revolutionist, Andrew founded Cultivating Community with the goal of bringing people together by redefining the way we participate in our food system.</p>

  <p>Rooted in the belief that everyone deserves a say in how their food is grown and where it comes from, he sees gardening as a powerful tool for building stronger, more connected, and self-reliant communities. </p>
  <p>Through Cultivating Community, Andrew hopes to make growing food more accessible, foster meaningful relationships, and empower people to take a more active role in shaping the food systems that sustain them.</p>
</section>

Cultivating Community is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit fostering connections around sustainable local food systems.
